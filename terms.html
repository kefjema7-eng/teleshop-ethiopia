Marketplace terms.

Draft template only. Have the final terms reviewed for your business and applicable Ethiopian law before public launch.

TeleShop is intended to connect customers with independent sellers. These starter terms describe the intended operating model and are not a substitute for reviewed legal terms.

Independent sellers

Sellers are responsible for accurate listings, lawful products, inventory, fulfilment, customer communication, and arranging delivery as described by the marketplace.

Orders and pricing

The server rechecks listed prices and available stock when an order is submitted. Delivery fees are not included in the item subtotal and must be confirmed by the seller. An order may remain pending until the seller confirms it.

Payments

Cash on delivery is the only enabled payment method in this release. Do not claim that Telebirr, Chapa, CBE Birr, cards, or bank payments are supported until a provider integration and verification flow are implemented and tested.

Cancellations and disputes

The operator must publish clear cancellation, returns, refund, counterfeit-product, dispute, and seller enforcement policies before launch. The current software does not implement a complete dispute/refund process.

Prohibited use

Users must not list unlawful or unsafe goods, impersonate others, manipulate orders, misuse customer data, or attempt to bypass access controls. The operator should define enforcement and appeal procedures.
