Our Story

From Harar to the future of Ethiopian commerce.

I'm a young technology entrepreneur from Harar, Ethiopia. I grew up around businesses run through hard work, trust, relationships, and conversations.

I started thinking about how technology could open new doors for local businesses — without forcing them to become something they are not.

TeleShop is built around that idea: create a digital environment where Ethiopian businesses can present themselves professionally, reach customers, manage their products, and grow.

01 · Where Harar, Ethiopia — a historic center of trade, craftsmanship and culture.
02 · Why Ethiopian businesses deserve technology designed around their reality.
03 · What A digital commerce platform connecting businesses and customers.
04 · The Goal Build from Ethiopia and create technology capable of reaching beyond Ethiopia.
