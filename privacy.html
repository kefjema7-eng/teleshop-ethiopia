Privacy notice.

Draft template only. The marketplace owner must review and adapt this notice to actual operations and applicable Ethiopian law before public launch.

This page explains the intended categories of data TeleShop may process. It is not a statement that every control described below is already implemented.

Information collected

Account details such as name and email; order details such as items, quantity, delivery address and phone number; seller store and product information; and basic technical/security logs may be processed to operate the marketplace.

How information is used

Information may be used to create accounts, display seller listings, fulfil orders, coordinate seller-arranged delivery, prevent abuse, provide customer support, and maintain marketplace operations.

Who receives order information

For an order, the relevant seller needs the customer's delivery and contact details to coordinate fulfilment. Do not collect or share information unrelated to an order.

Storage and security

TeleShop uses a database and access controls in this project, but security depends on deployment, configuration, backups, monitoring, and operational practices. Do not use real customer data until the production security review is complete.

Retention, access, and contact

The operator must define retention periods, account deletion and data access procedures, and a real privacy contact before launch. Replace this template with a reviewed policy.
